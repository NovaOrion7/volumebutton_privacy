1Introduction

Nova Orion ("we", "us") develops the Android app Nova Orion Volume Control (package com.novaorion.volumecontrol, the "App"). The App gives you on-screen volume controls, a floating button, profiles, widgets, a Quick Settings tile, an equalizer and volume boost, per-app volume, an on-screen assistant character and small mini-games. It is made for phones with broken or worn volume buttons.

This policy explains what information is handled, why, and what choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Data controller: Nova Orion — nova.orion.dev@gmail.com

2Information we collect

We (Nova Orion) do not run any servers and do not receive personal data about you. Third-party SDKs in the App may collect the following:

  • Device and ad data (Google AdMob, Unity LevelPlay and their ad networks): Android Advertising ID, device model, OS version, language, IP address (used to infer approximate country/region), and ad interactions such as impressions and clicks.
  • Consent status (Google UMP): your choices in the consent form.
  • Purchase data (Google Play Billing): if you buy Premium, Google tells the App whether the purchase is active. We never see your card or payment details.
  • Diagnostics: anonymous crash reports and statistics provided by Google Play, if you share usage and diagnostics through your device settings.

What we do not collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, files or messages.
  • Audio. The App has no microphone permission and never records anything.
  • Screen content or anything you type.
  • Precise (GPS) location.

3Data that stays on your device

The following are stored only on your phone, in the App's private storage. They are never sent to us:

  • Settings, theme and language, volume profiles, equalizer and boost settings, and widget settings.
  • Per-app volume settings. To show this list, the App reads the names and icons of apps that appear in your launcher. This list is used only on the device.
  • Floating button and assistant position, size and character choice; mini-game scores.
If Android backup is on, your device may include these settings in your Google account backup. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data deletes them.

5Accessibility service

The App includes an optional accessibility service. It is used only to perform these system actions when you tap a button in the App:

  • open the power menu,
  • lock the screen,
  • take a screenshot (saved by Android to your gallery, not by us).
The service is configured so it cannot read window content and cannot perform gestures. It does not see what you type or what is on your screen, and it does not collect or share any data. If you don't turn it on, everything else in the App keeps working.

8International transfers & retention

Google and Unity may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States, using safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We hold no data about you, so we have nothing to retain. Third parties keep data according to their own policies. On-device data is kept until you clear it or uninstall the App.

9Your rights & choices

  • Change ad consent: use the privacy options in the App's settings, or clear the App's data to see the consent form again.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Remove ads: buy Premium.
  • Permissions: revoke any optional permission (overlay, accessibility, device admin, Do Not Disturb) in system settings at any time.
  • Delete local data: Settings → Apps → Nova Orion Volume Control → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the App.

EEA/UK (GDPR): you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because we don't hold your personal data, requests about ad data are best sent to Google or Unity; we will help you with any request at the email below.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, delete, and opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising (deny consent or delete your Advertising ID), and you won't be discriminated against for using these rights.
